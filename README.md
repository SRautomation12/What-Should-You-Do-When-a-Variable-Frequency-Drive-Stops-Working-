# What-Should-You-Do-When-a-Variable-Frequency-Drive-Stops-Working-
Is a Drive Fault Interrupting Your Machine?
A drive can fail because of electrical problems, overheating, environmental conditions, worn components or trouble elsewhere in the connected system. Running the equipment without finding the cause can make things worse.
Why Does Proper Diagnosis Matter?
A systematic inspection shows whether the problem sits in the drive, its power supply, the motor, the wiring or the operating conditions. That tells you whether to repair, replace or fix something else.
Can Every Faulty Drive Be Repaired?
No. It depends on the fault, parts availability, the condition of the equipment and the cost of repair. A qualified technician should assess the unit before recommending the next step.
